Recent prediction by Daniel K. (author of AI 2027 Project) and his team at AI Futures Project, projects that we will have SAR (Superhuman AI Researcher: essentially the product of recursive self improvement, an AI that self heals, self replicates, self trains and self deploys instances of it based on its need) somewhere in 2027. This is a revised timeline, moved up from Daniel's original prediction of late 2028. This is upsetting. His team also believes that TED AI (Top Expert Dominating AI: AI that is able to perform and wipe out all white-collar jobs) will also arrive before 2028, which was previously predicted for 2029. Daniel had to move up his timelines because even his seemingly "most aggressive predictions" from the perspective of April 2025 are about 5 months behind as per the METR report, which states that Anthropic is able to have 1.7x improvement in research, which cannot only be attributed to human effort. This level of growth was projected for March 2027, but we are 5 months early, and many months late in asking for a gradual pause or a hard stop to RSI development. This is all very upsetting, both for those deeply embedded in the field, and most of us on the outside wanting to get a peek in. Hard to find reasons to still keep going without feeling like its too late for me, or any of us to make substantial changes.
